Chicken Karahi

About this Recipe

Cuisine: Pakistani

Difficulty: Easy

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 4

Calories per Serving: 420

Rating: 4.8 (68 reviews)

Ingredients

  • Chicken, cut into pieces
  • Tomatoes, chopped
  • Green chilies, sliced
  • Ginger, julienned
  • Garlic, minced
  • Coriander powder
  • Cumin powder
  • Red chili powder
  • Garam masala
  • Cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. In a wok (karahi), heat cooking oil and sauté minced garlic until golden brown.
  2. Add chicken pieces and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes, green chilies, ginger, and spices. Cook until tomatoes are soft.
  4. Cover and simmer until the chicken is tender and the oil separates from the masala.
  5. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with naan or rice.
