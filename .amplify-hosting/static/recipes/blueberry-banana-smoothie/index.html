Blueberry Banana Smoothie

About this Recipe

Cuisine: Smoothie

Difficulty: Easy

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 0 minutes

Servings: 1

Calories per Serving: 220

Rating: 4.8 (30 reviews)

Ingredients

  • Blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • Banana, peeled and sliced
  • Greek yogurt
  • Almond milk
  • Honey
  • Chia seeds (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a blender, combine blueberries, banana, Greek yogurt, almond milk, and honey.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add chia seeds for extra nutrition and blend briefly.
  4. Pour into a glass and enjoy this nutritious Blueberry Banana Smoothie!
