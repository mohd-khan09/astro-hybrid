Lebanese Falafel Wrap

About this Recipe

Cuisine: Lebanese

Difficulty: Easy

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 10 minutes

Servings: 2

Calories per Serving: 400

Rating: 4.7 (84 reviews)

Ingredients

  • Falafel balls
  • Whole wheat or regular wraps
  • Tomatoes, diced
  • Cucumbers, sliced
  • Red onions, thinly sliced
  • Lettuce, shredded
  • Tahini sauce
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Warm falafel balls according to package instructions.
  2. Place a generous serving of falafel in the center of each wrap.
  3. Top with diced tomatoes, sliced cucumbers, red onions, shredded lettuce, and fresh parsley.
  4. Drizzle with tahini sauce and wrap tightly.
  5. Enjoy this Lebanese Falafel Wrap filled with fresh and flavorful ingredients!
