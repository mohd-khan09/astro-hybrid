Mexican Street Corn (Elote)

About this Recipe

Cuisine: Mexican

Difficulty: Easy

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4

Calories per Serving: 180

Rating: 4.6 (2 reviews)

Ingredients

  • Corn on the cob
  • Mayonnaise
  • Cotija cheese, crumbled
  • Chili powder
  • Lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Grill or roast corn on the cob until kernels are charred.
  2. Brush each cob with mayonnaise, then sprinkle with crumbled Cotija cheese and chili powder.
  3. Serve with lime wedges for squeezing over the top.
  4. Enjoy this delicious and flavorful Mexican Street Corn!
