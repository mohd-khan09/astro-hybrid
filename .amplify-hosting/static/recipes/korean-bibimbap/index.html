Korean Bibimbap

About this Recipe

Cuisine: Korean

Difficulty: Medium

Prep Time: 30 minutes

Cook Time: 20 minutes

Servings: 2

Calories per Serving: 550

Rating: 4.9 (56 reviews)

Ingredients

  • Cooked white rice
  • Beef bulgogi (marinated and grilled beef slices)
  • Carrots, julienned and sautéed
  • Spinach, blanched and seasoned
  • Zucchini, sliced and grilled
  • Bean sprouts, blanched
  • Fried egg
  • Gochujang (Korean red pepper paste)
  • Sesame oil
  • Toasted sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Arrange cooked white rice in a bowl.
  2. Top with beef bulgogi, sautéed carrots, seasoned spinach, grilled zucchini, and blanched bean sprouts.
  3. Place a fried egg on top and drizzle with gochujang and sesame oil.
  4. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds before serving.
  5. Mix everything together before enjoying this delicious Korean bibimbap!
  6. Feel free to customize with additional vegetables or protein.
